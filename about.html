How I Got Here

Since childhood, I have been very interested in technologies and various gadgets.
Even when I found a device that I had not operated before, I always tried to get to know everything about it, as if I knew how to use it before.

I had a Java-compatible keypad phone, and I always liked to play around with it, modify it, and play various games on it.
Gradually, one step followed another, and I tried new things with various electronic devices.
One day, however, I received my first PC, and the interest in technologies became even bigger.

I started with gaming, and then I decided to make my own modifications of video games.
Moreover, I often downloaded various games and files from the Internet. Finally, this practice caused me to be hacked.

It changed my perspective on Security of our devices, as I didn’t have any idea about what was going on and what I should do.
It got me thinking about computer security, and my interest in cybersecurity was born.

Right now, being 16 years old, I study various programming languages, basics of computer science, and cyber security concepts.
I want to become an cyber security specialist. I understand that I still have much to learn until I reach my destination point,
but at least I am going in the right direction and learn something new every day.
